Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

clip2tiktok is a personal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Twitch and TikTok accounts.

What it accesses

With the owner's approval, the tool receives access tokens from Twitch and TikTok. It uses them to create and download Twitch clips and to upload videos to the owner's TikTok inbox. From TikTok it requests only basic profile information and permission to upload videos.

Where data is kept

Access tokens and downloaded videos are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Twitch and TikTok. No data is collected, sold or shared, and there is no tracking or analytics.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time by deleting the tool's tokens file, or by revoking the app in the Twitch and TikTok account settings.
